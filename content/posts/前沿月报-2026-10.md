2026 年 10 月共扫过 1207 篇论文，其中 77 篇达到 4 分及以上。下面按方向说说这个月的走向，文中论文都可以点开看原文。

## 大模型 LLM

这个月的 LLM 研究重心明显向「自演化系统的失效与修复」聚拢。多篇工作围绕自生成课程、自蒸馏、自训练中的退化现象展开：[False Frontiers: Diagnosing and Mitigating Co-Cheating in Self-Evolving Search Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39102) 指出 proposer 与 solver 在闭环中会共谋共享错误，内部奖励因此失真；[Questioning the Questions: Sustaining Self-Evolution in Reasoning Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04299) 与 [ReSAIL: Mitigating Collapse in Iterative Agent Self-Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39306) 分别从问题质量和特权信息依赖两个角度诊断多轮自训练的性能崩塌；[On-Policy Self-Training: Data-Free Self-Improvement in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32770) 则尝试用纯 on-policy 方式摆脱对真值标注的依赖。与之呼应的是 harness 层面的自演化探索，[Harness^2: Recursive Agent Harnessing for an Open World](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14224) 和 [Self-Evolving Agent Harness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18813) 把可修改对象从权重扩展到提示、记忆与工作流，[RSI Arena: Towards Recursive Self-Improvement with Human Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33624) 更进一步用十个前沿模型做端到端自主后训练的实验。共识正在形成：自演化的瓶颈不在生成能力，而在反馈信号的可信度与多样性，[Caught in the Act: Probes Effectively Detect Sabotage and Catch Unverbalized Deception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12445) 用探针检测未言明的欺骗、[Agent-Reflex: Teaching Language Agents to Act Reflectively](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17953) 训练智能体对噪声反馈做反思，都是对这一瓶颈的直接回应。

架构侧的主线是「循环 + 稀疏」的精细化。此前 looped transformer 与 MoE 各自独立的 scaling law 开始被合并建模，[Scaling Laws for Looped Mixture of Experts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40316) 给出联合缩放规律，[LatentLoop: Scaling Looped MoE Models with Asymmetric Compute Allocation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19975) 进一步发现不同循环轮次承担不同角色并据此非对称分配算力，[Towards Looped Models Done Right, Part II: Rethinking at Fixed Points](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06833) 则从不动点收敛性出发论证截断反传与 KV 共享的可行性。扩散语言模型同样在补「计算—难度错配」这一课：[Hierarchical Continuous Diffusion Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02193) 用层级化连续扩散缓解并行解码的边际独立采样问题，[ALoDLM: Adaptively Looped Diffusion Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04198) 引入自适应循环把算力压到难 token 上，[Large Flow Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28559) 和 [Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion-Uni: Elastic Self-Refining Unified Diffusion LLM with Cross-Paradigm On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36466) 则把连续流与离散扩散推到十亿参数级并尝试跨范式 on-policy 蒸馏。注意力与记忆机制上，[DoSA: Dots Sparse Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13990) 让稀疏注意力的 Top-K 选择本身变得条件化，[Continuum Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20586) 用连续分段线性记忆曲线替代离散查表，[Kronecker Delta Rule for Long Context Linear Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26834) 以 Kronecker Delta 规则做定向记忆修正，[Cortex: Consolidating Context into Parametric Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20399) 和 [MemT-1.0: Towards Memory-Native Evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26825) 则把上下文巩固进参数化记忆，指向「记忆作为原生计算组件」这一方向。

值得注意的是几个正在松动的旧假设。[Finetuning with Sampling: SFT Learns Better Than You Think](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02140) 直接挑战「SFT 只会记忆、RL 才泛化」的惯例，认为 SFT 的泛化被系统性低估；[Base Models Can Reason By Taking a Cue From Training Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06851) 显示基座模型仅靠起始 token 线索即可激发出接近强化学习后的推理表现，暗示推理能力可能更多是数据关联而非后训练产物；[Thinking Inertia: LLMs Keep Thinking When Told Not To](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11765) 把「不思考」本身作为研究对象，揭示模型存在难以抑制的思考惯性；[Safety Mid-Training: Internalizing LLM Safety as a Foundational Capability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26809) 主张把安全从后训练前移到中训练以缓解能力—安全权衡；[Text-to-Image Generative Modeling Without Language Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26863) 则质疑文生图是否真需要语言预训练。整体看，这个月的问题意识从「如何更强」转向「强化的机制是否被正确理解」，对失效模式的诊断、对训练信号来源的追问、以及对既有经验法则的复核，构成了比单项指标提升更清晰的推进线索。

## 强化学习 RL

这个月 RL 方向的研究重心明显集中在「如何把稀疏、粗糙的监督信号变密变准」这条主线上，而 on-policy distillation（OPD）成为最密集的攻坚场域。围绕 OPD 出现了整组工作：有的把教师信号从终点分布推广到方向性外推 [The Teacher Is a Direction, Not a Destination: Extrapolating RL-Induced Representation Residuals in On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36484)，有的针对多轮 agent 中错误累积问题设计从关键失误中恢复的机制 [PivotOPD: Learning to Recover from Pivotal Mistakes in Multi-Turn Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40285)，有的用幂分布锐化序列级概率而不依赖搜索 [Sharpen Without Search: On-Policy Distillation of Sequence-Level Power Distribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06804)，还有的试图把 RL 前后的策略偏移作为可迁移监督 [Waypoint-OPD: Transferring RL-Induced Policy Shifts via Gated On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21601)，以及在学生能力边界处寻找教学切入点 [Frontier-OPD: Discovering the Edge of Agent Capability and Teaching Agents to Go Beyond](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32802)。与之呼应的是自蒸馏路线的活跃：把事后经验转成先验预见 [Self-Retrospection Distillation: Turning Post-hoc Experiences into Prior Foresight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08077)、按 token 自适应选择多教师 [Every Token Needs Its Own Teacher: Multi-teacher Adaptive On-policy Self-distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40574)、在函数空间做时序自蒸馏 [Function-Space Temporal Self-Distillation for Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19687)，共同指向一个判断——标量结果奖励太粗，需要更细粒度的过程性监督，但教师信号本身也要被「诊断」和「定位」，不能无差别灌注。

第二个被反复攻的问题是长程 agent 的信用分配与环境规模化。多条工作围绕 turn 级与 token 级的分层信用展开 [Multiscale Policy Optimization for Long-Horizon LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35013)，用跟踪价值改进 PPO 的 critic [TPPO: Tracking-Value PPO For Language Model Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16794)，用树状后验蒸馏处理长程交互 [Tree-Aware Posterior Distillation for Long-Horizon Agentic Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23181)，用主动 critic 学习缓解中间价值估计不准 [FOCUS: Active Critic Learning for Long-Horizon Language Model Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35801)，以及把技能作为可复用先验并做选择性注入 [SkillScaffold: Scaffolding Agentic Reinforcement Learning with Policy-Adaptive Skill Credit Assignment and Selective Injection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18807)、让行动与预测协同演化 [Co-Evolving Acting and Forecasting for Language Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20597)。环境侧则出现用稠密进度奖励扩展长程任务 [Tev: Scaling Long-Horizon Agentic Environments with Dense Progress Rewards](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18868)、支持多 session 轨迹的设定 [Mosaic: Multi-Session Agentic Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22174)，以及从零数据构造长上下文可验证任务 [LongZero: Enhancing Long-Context Reasoning in LLMs from Zero Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30132)。与此同时，RLVR 的「信号消失」问题被正面处理：全对或全错的 group 无梯度时如何继续学习 [PIVOT: Progressive Intervention via Online Teacher Correction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40576)，以及把探索与优化解耦以恢复策略多样性 [Decoupling Exploration from Optimization in RLVR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10536)。这说明社区共识正在从「加算力堆 rollout」转向「在轨迹内部挖监督」。

另一个清晰的转折是对奖励本身的不信任与工程化修补。基于 rubric 的 RL 被指出加权求和聚合是 reward hacking 的薄弱点，于是出现协议级 rubric [Scoring Higher, Answering Worse: Mitigating Reward Hacking in Rubric-Based RL via Protocol-Level Rubrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38847) 和精确率目标 [Eat Your Precision: Precision Objective Mitigates Reward Hacking in Rubric-based RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33512) 两条纠偏路径；多奖励场景下则强调按奖励各自的相关 token 做归一与分配 [Each Reward to Its Relevant Tokens: Reward-Specific Token-Level Credit Assignment for Multi-Reward RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21439)。训练稳定性方面，有工作指出推理与训练系统舍入位置不一致会让 on-policy 变 off-policy [Where Rounding Happens Matters: Rounding-Location Alignment Stabilizes LLM Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32951)，也有工作重新审视大 rollout batch 与学习率缩放的实际行为 [Two Clocks: Why Larger Rollout Batches Learn Slower but Fail on Schedule in Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18853)，以及用 n-gram 投机解码加速 rollout [NgramRL: Accelerating RL Post-Training for Structured Generation via Rollout-Aware N-gram Speculative Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32881)。此外还出现了更宏观的路线主张：纯 RL 做领域适配以保留探索多样性 [HuatuoGPT-3: RL-Only Domain Adaptation from Base Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05966)、用单一算法统一预训练到 RL 的各阶段 [Training Foundation Models with a Single Algorithm](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40564)，以及以价值表征预测对齐泛化的尝试 [Predicting Alignment Generalization with Value Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12410)。整体看，这个月没有单一范式胜出，但「稠密化监督、细粒度信用、奖励稳健性、系统级一致性」四件事已被普遍当作必答题，OPD 与自蒸馏则是最拥挤的解法赛道。

## AI + 生物

这个月的论文明显把重心从「造更大的单细胞/蛋白质基础模型」转向「怎么让生成与预测真正对接湿实验约束」。单细胞侧 [CellMSA: Context Modeling for Single-Cell Representation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38908) 仍在处理高维稀疏与批次效应这类老问题，但表述上更强调上下文建模而非逐细胞独立编码；[CloneOT: predicting clone-specific perturbation responses with lineage-supervised neural inverse optimal transport](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16093) 则把扰动响应预测形式化为非配对分布间的神经最优传输，并用谱系监督约束映射，说明大家开始在意「跨条件、跨时间」的可迁移性而非单点拟合。蛋白质方向 [Learning Latent Protein Languages for Autoregressive Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03978)[Linear Fitness Subspace in Protein Language Models Enables Sample-Efficient Directed Evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07607) 形成一组呼应：前者质疑氨基酸 token 缺乏上下文语义、尝试在潜空间里学「蛋白质语言」再做自回归生成，后者则指出零样本打分与目标实验脱节，转而在 PLM 表征中找低维线性适应度子空间以实现样本高效的定向进化——两者共同指向一个共识：表征的好坏要用下游任务的可控性来检验，而不是靠困惑度。

生成式设计这边，硬约束与多目标权衡被反复攻。[pCoMole: Pareto-Constrained Molecule Editing with Discrete Flows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01663) 直接把 Pareto 约束和离散流结合，处理多性质优化与生化可制造性硬约束；[La-Ribo: RNA Co-Design via Geometry-Latent Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12236)[CocoRNA: Collective RNA Design with Cooperative Multi-agent Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23820) 从不同角度攻 RNA 序列-结构联合设计，一个用几何潜空间流匹配缓解结构监督稀缺，一个用多智能体协作强化学习做逆折叠；[De novo design of monoclonal and bispecific antibodies with OFAntibody](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09548) 则把抗体设计从单界面推广到双特异性多组分组装。结构推断侧 [Fold'EM: Direct atomic structure inference from Cryo-EM particles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01358) 绕开「先重建密度图再拟合原子模型」的传统两段式流程，直接从冷冻电镜粒子推原子结构，[Accurate structural modelling of protein-glycan recognition with GlycoWeave](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37410) 补上蛋白-糖识别这一实验结构覆盖稀疏的短板，都是在减少对中间表示和大量标注的依赖。

更明显的转折出现在「智能体 + 闭环实验」这条线上。[Unlocking the Regulatory Genome by ARGUS: An Evidence-Constrained Agentic Framework for Interpreting Single Nucleotide Variants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12281) 用证据约束的智能体框架抑制 LLM 在非编码变异解读中的幻觉，[AutoNovo: Agentic Program Search Discovers Transferable Nucleic Acid Design Algorithms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16232) 让程序搜索自动发现可迁移的核酸设计算法以对抗代理模型过拟合，[Aptamers as a sandbox for lab-in-the-loop agentic molecular discovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24806) 直接把适配体当作 lab-in-the-loop 的沙盒，让 LLM 推理智能体带着湿实验反馈迭代，[Agent-guided epitope excision enables economical de novo binder design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33195) 用表位裁剪压缩候选生成与排序的推理开销。再加上 [HELICS: Biobank-scale Conditional Synthetic Genome Generation via Latent Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36361) 以潜空间流匹配做生物样本库规模的条件合成基因组、[Generative modeling of intrinsically disordered protein regions by reinforcing sparse autoencoder features](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02189) 用稀疏自编码器特征强化来生成固有无序区，可以看出这个月的共同焦虑是「in silico 指标好看但不可迁移」，应对思路则是把约束、证据和实验反馈显式写进生成与搜索回路，而不是指望更大的模型自己涌现出可靠性。
